Australia's Agricultural Landscape

Exploring how Australia's land is used for grazing, cropping, horticulture and other agricultural activities.

01 - THE BIG PICTURE

More than half of Australia's land is classified for agricultural use

Agricultural land covers a substantial share of Australia, but that land is distributed very differently across grazing, cropping, horticulture and other agricultural activities.

AUSTRALIA'S LAND

54.5%

of Australia's land falls within agricultural-use classes.

This includes grazing native vegetation, grazing modified pastures, irrigated pastures, cropping, horticulture and other intensive agricultural uses.

02 - WHAT MAKES UP AGRICULTURAL LAND?

Grazing dominates Australia's agricultural land use

Australia's agricultural land is not evenly divided. Grazing occupies the overwhelming majority, while cropping and horticulture use much smaller areas.

04 - HOW HAS AGRICULTURAL LAND CHANGED?

Cropping expanded by 32% while grazing declined slightly

Australia's agricultural footprint shifted unevenly between 2010-11 and 2020-21. Cropping recorded the largest absolute expansion, gaining about 10.4 million hectares, while grazing declined by about 9.5 million hectares. Horticulture and intensive agriculture also increased, although from much smaller land bases.

A DECADE OF CHANGE

+32.4% Cropping +10.39 M ha
-2.5% Grazing -9.5 M ha
+24.8% Horticulture +0.16 M ha
+35.0% Intensive agriculture +0.036 M ha

05 - WHERE DID THE LAND SHIFT?

The largest net shift was from grazing into cropping

The expansion of cropping was closely matched by a major net shift from grazing-related land into cropping. Smaller shifts also occurred towards horticulture and intensive agriculture.

06 - WHERE IS AGRICULTURE MOST DOMINANT?

Agricultural-use land covers around three-quarters of Queensland and New South Wales

Agricultural land is not distributed evenly across Australia. Queensland and New South Wales have the highest shares of their landscapes classified within agricultural-use categories, while Tasmania and the Australian Capital Territory have much lower shares.

HIGHEST SHARES

Queensland 79.2%
New South Wales 76.4%
Victoria 56.2%

This contrast shows how unevenly agricultural land is distributed across Australia's states and territories.

08 - HOW DOES THE AGRICULTURAL MIX DIFFER BY STATE?

Grazing dominates agricultural land in every state, while Victoria has the most balanced mix

The composition of agricultural-use land differs considerably across Australia. Grazing accounts for the majority of agricultural land in every state and territory, but Victoria stands out because cropping occupies almost as much agricultural land as grazing.

MOST BALANCED MIX

Victoria
Grazing 53.1%
Cropping 44.1%
Horticulture 2.3%

Victoria has the most even split between grazing and cropping, unlike most other states where grazing clearly dominates.

Queensland, Western Australia and the Northern Territory remain strongly grazing-oriented, creating a clear contrast with Victoria.

09 - WHERE ARE AUSTRALIA'S BROADACRE CROPS GROWN?

Broadacre crops follow very different geographic patterns across Australia

CROP PATTERNS ACROSS AUSTRALIA

Wheat has the broadest geographic footprint, with large planted areas across Western Australia, South Australia, New South Wales and Victoria.

Canola is strongest in the southern states, particularly Western Australia, Victoria and New South Wales.

Chickpeas are more concentrated in eastern Australia, while sorghum and cotton are much more strongly centred on Queensland and New South Wales.

12 - HOW HAS THE VALUE OF HORTICULTURE CHANGED?

Fruit and vegetables increased in value while wine grapes remained below 2020-21 levels

Australia's horticultural industries have followed different economic paths since 2020-21. Fruit and vegetables recorded substantial growth, while wine grapes declined before beginning a partial recovery.

2020-21 TO 2024-25

Fruit +25.3%
Vegetables +22.9%
Nuts +38.5%
Wine grapes -27.5%

Fruit remained the largest horticultural group by farm-gate value, reaching approximately A$7.34 billion in 2024-25.

Nut values were more volatile, falling sharply in 2022-23 before recovering strongly.

13 - WHICH HORTICULTURAL CROPS EXPANDED THEIR GROWING AREA?

Macadamia area expanded while banana area contracted

Selected horticultural crops followed different land-allocation patterns between 2023-24 and 2024-25. Macadamia growing area increased, avocado area remained almost unchanged, while banana area declined.

AREA CHANGE

Macadamias +5.3%
Avocados +0.1%
Bananas -4.6%

Macadamia growing area increased by about 2,356 hectares, the largest expansion among the selected crops.

Avocado area remained almost unchanged, while banana growing area decreased by around 620 hectares.
